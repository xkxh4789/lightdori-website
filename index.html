LightDoriSheet

01Sheet & export

Tick the rows, get the paperwork

Pick fixtures in the Sheet and press Export. Channel, dimmer and address hookups, the instrument schedule and the focus chart come straight out.

  • A header filled from your creditsDirector, designer and venue go on top of every page.
  • Three table styles, any paperClassic, Grid or Compact, on the page size you print.
  • PDF, data, XML and MVREvery PDF you make waits in the Store.

02Blocking

Where everyone goes, cue by cue

The cues you mark on the script become the steps of the blocking. Put performers and set pieces on the stage plan, move them at each cue, and every move stays as an arrow.

  • Beats and follow spotsSplit a cue when things move more than once, and say whom each spot picks up.
  • At a glance, who is on, the reportSee the busiest areas of the stage and how far each performer walks.
  • An A4 chart and a phone imagePrint the blocking chart, or send one tall image to the company chat.

03Colour

See the light before you pick the gel

Shine any of 526 LEE and Rosco filters on a small 3D stage. Bring the dimmer down and it warms the way a tungsten lamp does.

  • Transmission, shift and spectrumFrom the makers' own published data.
  • The closest gel in the other brandLEE to Rosco and back, in one click.
  • The same library in the SheetColour cells open it as their picker.

04Magic Sheet

Change the Sheet, the magic sheet follows

Channels fall into cells by purpose or position, laid out in the shape of the stage. Each bubble wears its gel colour and points where its light goes.

  • Pick a layout, then adjustDrag channels into place and turn them with the round handle.
  • Ground plan and gel legendThe plan sits in grey behind the cells.
  • A3 or A4, ready to printWith the same header as your paperwork.
