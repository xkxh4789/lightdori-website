Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

1. What we collect

LightDori collects only what an account and a shared session need to work:

  • Account information — name, email address, and a password stored as a salted hash. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Licence information — the licence key attached to your account and its activation status.
  • Collaboration data — session names, membership, and the project data you choose to share with a session, along with its edit history.
  • Support correspondence — anything you send us by email.

The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or the contents of projects you keep on your own computer. Project files stay on your machine unless you deliberately share them through a collaboration session.

2. Why we use it

To create and authenticate your account, to run collaboration sessions, to validate licences, and to answer your support requests. We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it to anyone.

3. Update checks

LightDori asks GitHub whether a newer release exists. That request carries the app version and the usual network information any web request carries. It does not include your account details or your project data.

4. Where your information is stored

Account and session records are held using Supabase, a hosted database service whose servers are located outside the Republic of Korea. Using LightDori's account features therefore involves transferring your personal information overseas.

  • Transferred to: Supabase, Inc.
  • Country: United States
  • When: at the moment you create an account, sign in, or use a collaboration session
  • What: the account, licence, and collaboration data described in section 1
  • Purpose: database hosting and authentication for LightDori
  • Retention: for as long as your account exists, then deleted as described in section 5

You may refuse this transfer, but the account and collaboration features cannot be provided without it. LightDori's offline features remain available.

5. How long we keep it

Account information is kept until you delete your account, after which it is removed without undue delay. Collaboration history is kept for the life of the session and removed when the session is deleted. Where a law requires us to keep a record for longer, we keep only that record for the period the law requires.

6. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop processing it. Write to the contact below and we will respond without undue delay. You may also complain to the Korea Internet & Security Agency Privacy Call Centre (118) or the Personal Information Protection Commission.

7. Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Traffic between LightDori and our servers is encrypted in transit. Database access is restricted by row-level security so that one account cannot read another's records.

8. Children

LightDori is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 14. We do not knowingly create accounts for them.

9. Person responsible

  • Email: xkxh4789@gmail.com

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above before it takes effect.
